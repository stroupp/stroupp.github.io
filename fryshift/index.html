Fry Shift – Support

A fry stand by day, an odd night shift after dark. By Joe's Game Box. Questions or problems? Write to serkan_bicer38@hotmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

How do I play the day?

Tap the potato crate, lift the fries when they turn golden, then tap the carton the customer wants: it goes straight to them. Reach the day's coin goal for one to three stars. Missed it? Try again; you keep the coins you earned.

How do I play the night?

Look around the counter and the three cameras. When something moves, vanishes, appears, changes, flickers or a visitor shows up, open REPORT, pick the place and the kind. Unreported oddities raise your fear; last until 6 AM.

I bought something but it did not arrive

Open the shop and tap Restore purchases. If it is still missing, write to us with the date of the purchase.
