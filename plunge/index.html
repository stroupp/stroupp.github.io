Plunge – Support

The rarer your answer, the deeper you dive. By Joe's Game Box. Questions or problems? Write to serkan_bicer38@hotmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

How do I play?

A prompt appears, for example "Name a fruit". Type one answer and press the arrow. Every correct answer counts; the less known it is, the deeper Pip dives. Air runs out while you think, and rare answers give some back. Reach the depth goal for one to three stars.

My answer was not accepted

Each prompt has a long list of answers in your language; small spelling mistakes are forgiven. If you think a correct answer is missing, write to us with the prompt and the answer.

I bought something but it did not arrive

Open the shop and tap Restore purchases. If it is still missing, write to us with the date of the purchase.

Where do the answers come from?

The answer lists come from Wikidata (CC0) and how well known an answer is from QRank (public domain, Wikimedia page views).
