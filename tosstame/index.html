Toss & Tame – Support

A snack-tossing animal game by Joe's Game Box. Questions or problems? Write to serkan_bicer38@hotmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

How do I play?

Hold anywhere on the field and release when the meter is in the yellow zone: the catapult throws a snack. Animals run to where it lands; tap one, then tap again when the shrinking ring meets the white circle to tame it. Tamed animals live in your sanctuary and earn coins.

I am out of energy

Every throw uses one energy. One comes back every 12 minutes and an empty bar is full again in 4 hours. Tap the energy chip (bottom right) to watch an optional video for +10 energy. Meanwhile you can pet your animals, decorate the sanctuary or train.

An animal left my sanctuary

Animals are guests for three days; then they go home, leave a farewell gift and stay in your album. Animals with five hearts (tap the heart bubbles in the sanctuary), shiny animals and your companion stay for good.

I bought something but it did not arrive / I have a new phone

Open Settings (gear, top left) or the bottom of the Shop and tap Restore purchases. No Ads, Double Income and the Starter Pack come back on every device with the same Apple ID or Google account.

Ads and privacy

Read the privacy policy. Players in Europe can change their ad choices in Settings.
