Barocue – Privacy Policy

Developer: Joe's Game Box (Serkan Biçer) · Effective date: 7 November 2026 · Contact: serkan_bicer38@hotmail.com

This policy explains what the iOS app Barocue does with information. In short: there is no account, no advertising and no tracking. Your diary stays on your iPhone. To show a forecast, the app sends your approximate location to a weather service.

1. What the app processes

InformationWhere it goesWhy
Approximate location: latitude and longitude rounded to 2 decimals (about 1 km) Sent to the weather API of the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (api.met.no) To get the air pressure forecast for that place
Diary entries: how you feel, symptoms, medicine names, notes, time, and the pressure at that time Stored only on your iPhoneTo show your diary and your own patterns
Settings: unit, alert threshold, language, chosen city, stored pressure history Stored only on your iPhoneTo remember your choices and draw the chart

If you choose a city instead of using your location, only that city's rounded coordinates are sent. Location permission is optional.

2. What the app does not do

We do not collect your name, e-mail address, contacts, health records or advertising identifier. The app does not use Apple Health (HealthKit), contains no ads and no analytics or tracking tools, and we do not sell or share any data. There is no server of ours that stores your information.

3. Third parties

MET Norway provides the weather data and receives the rounded coordinates of each forecast request, together with the technical data any internet request carries (such as the IP address). MET Norway's own privacy terms apply to that request. Apple processes subscriptions and in-app purchases; we never receive payment details.

4. Notifications

Pressure alerts are local notifications planned on your iPhone. No push service or notification token is used.

5. Your choices and deleting data

You can turn off location access and notifications in the iPhone Settings app. To delete everything, use Settings → Delete all data in the app or delete the app. Because there is no account or server copy, this removes all your data. Pro users can export their diary as a CSV file.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.

Barocue is not a medical device and does not diagnose, predict or treat any condition.

Barocue – Gizlilik Politikası

Geliştirici: Joe's Game Box (Serkan Biçer) · Yürürlük tarihi: 7 Kasım 2026 · İletişim: serkan_bicer38@hotmail.com

Barocue'da hesap, reklam ve izleme yoktur. Günlüğün yalnızca iPhone'unda tutulur. Tahmini gösterebilmek için uygulama yaklaşık konumunu (2 ondalığa yuvarlanmış, yaklaşık 1 km) Norveç Meteoroloji Enstitüsü'nün (MET Norway) hava servisine gönderir; şehir seçersen yalnızca o şehrin koordinatları gönderilir.

Ad, e-posta, rehber, sağlık kaydı veya reklam kimliği toplanmaz; Apple Sağlık (HealthKit) kullanılmaz; analiz veya izleme aracı yoktur; veriler satılmaz veya paylaşılmaz. Abonelik ve satın alımları Apple işler; ödeme bilgileri bize ulaşmaz. Basınç uyarıları telefonda planlanan yerel bildirimlerdir.

Konum ve bildirim izinlerini iPhone Ayarlar'dan kapatabilirsin. Tüm verileri uygulamadaki Ayarlar → Tüm verileri sil ile ya da uygulamayı silerek kaldırabilirsin. Uygulama 13 yaş altı çocuklara yönelik değildir.
