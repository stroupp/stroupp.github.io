How do the alerts work?

Barocue checks the air pressure forecast for your location and finds windows where the pressure changes by more than your threshold within 6 hours. It then sends a notification 1, 3 or 6 hours before the change starts (Settings → Alerts). Quiet hours move alerts out of 10 PM–7 AM.

I don't get alerts

Check that notifications for Barocue are allowed in the iPhone Settings app (Settings → Notifications → Barocue) and that Pressure alerts is on in Barocue's Settings. Open the app now and then: each time it opens, the alerts for the coming days are planned again. You can send yourself a test alert from Settings.

Where does the forecast come from?

From the Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), under the CC BY 4.0 licence. Values are sea-level pressure, resampled to hourly steps. You can use your location or choose a city in Settings.

Restore a purchase / new phone

Open Settings → Barocue Pro and tap Restore purchases. Pro comes back on every device signed in with the same Apple Account.

Cancel or change a subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple: iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Barocue, or Settings → Manage subscription in the app. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period to avoid the next renewal. For refunds, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

Delete my data

Your diary and settings are stored only on your iPhone. Use Settings → Delete all data, or delete the app. See the privacy policy.

Not medical advice. Barocue is not a medical device and does not diagnose, predict or treat any condition. Pressure alerts are weather information. Talk to your doctor about headaches, migraine and medicine.

Uyarılar gelmiyor

iPhone Ayarlar → Bildirimler → Barocue'dan bildirimlere izin verildiğini ve uygulamada Basınç uyarılarının açık olduğunu kontrol et. Uygulamayı ara sıra aç; her açılışta önümüzdeki günlerin uyarıları yeniden planlanır. Ayarlar'dan deneme uyarısı gönderebilirsin.

Satın alımı geri yükleme / yeni telefon

Ayarlar → Barocue Pro → Satın alımları geri yükle. Pro, aynı Apple hesabıyla giriş yapılan her cihazda geri gelir.

Aboneliği iptal etme

iPhone Ayarlar → adın → Abonelikler → Barocue ya da uygulamada Ayarlar → Aboneliği yönet. Yenilenmemesi için dönem bitiminden en az 24 saat önce iptal et.
