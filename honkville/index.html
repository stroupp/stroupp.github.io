Honkville – Support

A traffic puzzle by Joe's Game Box. Questions or problems? Write to serkan_bicer38@hotmail.com and we will reply as soon as we can.

How do I play?

Tap a car and it drives in the direction of its arrow. It can only leave through a gate of its own color. Clear every car to win.

I am stuck on a level

Use a hint, undo your last move, or call the tow truck or the police from the bar at the bottom. If no move is left, the game offers a free undo.

I bought something but it did not arrive / I have a new phone

Open Settings (gear, top left) and tap Restore purchases. Remove Ads and the Starter Pack come back on every device with the same Apple ID or Google account.

Ads and privacy

Read the privacy policy. Players in Europe can change their ad choices in Settings.
